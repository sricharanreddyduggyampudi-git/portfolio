The Process

PLOTS OF THE DESIGN LORE

01

EMPATHIZE

Understand before you create. Research people, behaviour, context, and opportunity.

02

DEFINE

Find the problem behind the problem. Turn research into insights, criteria, and direction.

03

IDEATE

Make the obvious less obvious. Explore possibilities through sketching, concepts, and experimentation.

04

PROTOTYPE

Put the idea in your hands. Turn concepts into physical and digital experiments.

05

ITERATE

Let reality argue back. Test, learn, challenge, and refine.

06

REFLECT

Every project leaves something behind. Capture what worked, what failed, and what comes next.

Vivus

(01) Brief
Role: Furniture Design
Biomorphic Modeling
Timeline: 2025
What happens when living marine biology is translated into the sculptural language of soft seating?

Sea anemones create their distinctive visual signature through hundreds of soft, cylindrical tentacles emerging together in a dense organic field. Rather than reproducing the organism literally as a novelty shape, Vivus abstracts its essential qualities—vertical growth, continuous repetition, soft radii, and rhythmic enveloping boundaries—into a progressive sofa system. A sequence of CNC-milled structural foam ribs wraps continuously around a solid walnut datum.

(02) Overview
SCULPTURAL, ORGANIC, ENVELOPING. A continuous field of upholstered vertical ribs anchored to a solid walnut datum, translating marine biomorphism into tactile ergonomic luxury.
Fig 01: Front Elevation
Front structural elevation: The dense rhythmic sequence of upholstered ribs rises organically from the walnut datum to form the deep seat, contoured backrest, and integrated armrests.
Fig 02: Seam & Curvature
Material & seam transition: Close-up inspection of the tactile bouclé textile wrapping each independent rib core, emphasizing tight seam tolerances and organic curvature.
Fig 03-04: Spatial Architecture
Spatial architecture: Top-angled envelope curvature showcasing deep lumbar compliance, paired with interior lighting study highlighting organic contrast against stone and timber.
Fig 05: 3D Turntable Simulation
3D turntable visualization examining surface light falloff, tactile textile depth, and structural foam deflection under ergonomic load.
Fig 06-07: Silhouette & Clearance
Form generation & rear silhouette: Rib spacing iterations evaluating user clearance, and full 360° freestanding sculptural presence for open-plan residential lounges.

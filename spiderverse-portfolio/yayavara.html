Timeline: 2023 (Academic Project)
How will everyday furniture adapt when frequent urban relocation is the default reality of modern life?

Urban professionals and students relocate frequently, yet conventional furniture remains heavy, prone to permanent damage when dismantled, and reliant on metal fasteners that strip after repeated assembly cycles. Yāyāvara solves this systemic friction through a complete 4-piece home suite—bed platform, ergonomic workspace desk, matching chair, and modular tiered shelving—that ships flat, carries easily, and locks together securely in minutes without a single screw, bolt, or tool.

(02) Overview
MODULAR, HARDWARE-FREE, COMPACT. Precision CNC-milled birch plywood engineered with interlocking friction slots, delivering zero-tool assembly and consolidating into a single portable bundle for effortless relocation.

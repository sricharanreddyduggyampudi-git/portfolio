About Me

I am an Industrial Designer driven by a maker’s curiosity — exploring the space between abstract ideas and physical reality to build products people can hold, test, and rely on. Grounded in human-centered research, hands-on prototyping, and Design for Manufacturing (DFM/DFA), my work spans physical hardware, modular furniture, assistive biomechanics, and scalable digital design systems. I believe in putting concepts into the hands of users early, letting reality challenge every assumption, and designing with purpose, craftsmanship, and empathy.

StrikeFly

(01) Brief
Role: Soft Goods Design
Pattern Drafting
Domain: Combat Sports Gear
Bespoke Leather Craft
CMF: Full-Grain Moroccan Goat Hide
Bonded Nylon & Brass
Timeline: 2025
Boxing gloves rarely have a place of their own. High-grade sparring gear deserves dedicated craft.
Most combat athletes transport expensive 12oz–16oz leather gloves inside generic nylon gym sacks or oversized duffles, where sweat-soaked padding is crushed and moisture is trapped against training clothes. StrikeFly is a dedicated leather carry engineered specifically around the volume, breathability, and everyday journey of boxing gloves and hand wraps. Inspired by the agile flight and structural venation of the dragonfly (Anisoptera), StrikeFly translates athletic utility through artisanal leathercraft—creating an enduring ritual before and after the fight.
(02) Overview
TACTILE, BREATHABLE, ARTISAN. Hand-drafted full-grain goat leather featuring a wide-mouth drawstring cinch and dragonfly wing topstitching tailored specifically to the dimensions of sparring gloves.
Primary Hide: Full-Grain Vegetable-Tanned Goat Leather (Moroccan Pebble Grain)
Reinforcement: Bonded High-Strength Nylon + Laser-Etched Wing Venation
Closure & Carry: Braided Core Drawstring Cinch + Dual Low-Profile Shoulder Straps
Fig 01-02: Form & Carry
Form & on-body fit: Handcrafted vertical silhouette paired with an ergonomic sling carry. The low-profile profile rests flush against the torso during motorcycle and urban commutes, preventing annoying bag swing.
(03) Research & Ritual

Engineered for the Everyday Journey of Boxing Gloves

Sparring gloves are unique: they demand frequent venting to prevent bacteria buildup, yet need protective containment when in transit. Field research across combat gyms identified three distinct friction points across the fighter’s routine:

01 / Packing Access Bulky 12oz–16oz gloves catch on standard zip bags and narrow gym sacks. StrikeFly introduces an oversized cylindrical throat for effortless drop-in stowing.
02 / In-Transit Mobility Heavy duffles shift on bikes and public transit. Dual long shoulder straps distribute weight evenly across the back, hugging the spine without bouncing.
03 / Post-Training Hygiene Synthetic plastic bags trap sweat and degrade leather palm liners. Natural goat hide offers porous breathability, venting moisture immediately after sparring.
The Fighter's Ritual Sequence
Pack Gear & Wraps → Low-Profile Transit → Gym Arrival & Uncinch → Sparring Session → Post-Fight Breathable Stow
“First, it is an intention. Then a behavior. Then a habit. Then a practice. Then second nature.”
Fig 03: Aperture
Aperture mechanics: Wide-mouth top throat allows frictionless insertion of bulky 16oz gloves without catching wrist wraps, collapsing tightly under single-pull cinch tension.
(04) Material & Architecture

Goat Hide Performance & Dragonfly Wing Venation

Rather than decorative styling, every material choice and stitch line serves structural load distribution. The geometric venation of dragonfly wings was analyzed for its lightweight rigidity, inspiring the laser-etched topstitching on the rear spine.

Attribute Engineering & Craft Specification
Moroccan Goat Leather Selected over cowhide for superior tensile tear strength at lower gauge weight. Natural pebble grain masks abrasive scuffs and develops a rich patina over years of gym use.
Biomimetic Wing Spine Laser-etched and machine-topstitched wing venation pattern anchors the strap anchor points, diffusing tensile pull across the entire back panel.
Drawstring Closure High-density braided core cord passing through reinforced brass eyelets provides swift, snag-free cinch action with gloves still partially on.
Fig 04-05: CMF & Spine
Materiality & structure: Natural pebble-grain Moroccan goat leather tested for sweat resilience, anchored by geometric spine topstitching inspired by dragonfly wing venation.
